Restaurant

Classic modern Italian cuisine in the heart of Frankfurt's Westend. Spacious, elegant ambiance. In summer, a large, sheltered and bright garden area invites you to enjoy extended lunch and dinner.

Regular guests appreciate the personal service and discreet atmosphere.

Just a few minutes' walk from the trade fair and the Alte Oper, the Ristorante "La Scuderia" offers a charming Italian ambiance. In good weather, you can dine in the enchanting garden.

Cuisine

Massimo Desortes has been leading the kitchen since 2005. The focus is on traditional, reliable and artisanal Italian culinary art.

"Clear product-focused cuisine is an important branch of our restaurant culture."

Jürgen Dollase, FAS

Wines

Our Italian wine cellar has been compiled with great dedication and passion and has been continuously growing since 2005.
